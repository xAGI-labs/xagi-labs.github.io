Ram Japa Counter

Support & Privacy

A private, offline japa companion for counting each spoken Ram or Raam toward a mala of 108.

Support

For help with microphone permissions, on-device recognition, counting, history, or another app issue, email saurav@xagi.in. Typical response time is 2–3 business days.

When contacting support, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. You never need to send a microphone recording.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026

Ram Japa Counter is designed to work completely offline. The developer does not collect, receive, sell, or share personal data from the app.

Microphone and speech processing

The app requests microphone and speech-recognition permission solely to detect the spoken mantra Ram or Raam. Audio and recognition are processed on your iPhone. Raw microphone audio is not uploaded, transmitted, or stored by the app.

Information stored on your device

Your current count, today’s count, lifetime count, completed malas, preferences, and lightweight session history are stored locally on your iPhone. The app has no account, cloud sync, or backend. Removing the app may remove this locally stored information according to iOS behavior.

No analytics, advertising, or tracking

The app contains no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking technology, account system, or third-party data collection service. It does not make network requests and continues to function in airplane mode.

Apple services and permissions

iOS manages microphone and speech-recognition permissions and may manage the availability of Apple’s on-device recognition resources. The developer does not receive audio, transcripts, or permission data from Apple.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the revised version and effective date will be posted here. For support or privacy questions, contact saurav@xagi.in.
