Privacy policy

Your music. Your choices.

Updated 7 October 2026

Who operates OpenMusic

OpenMusic: AI Music Studio is operated by xAGI Labs. This policy applies to the Android app, OpenMusic web app, and its service. Contact saurav@xagi.in with privacy questions. Effective 7 October 2026.

Information we handle

An invitation account has an internal user ID, account label, hashed access credential, and credit balance. We store prompts, lyrics, input settings and any public HTTPS media URLs you submit; generation/job identifiers and results; track titles and audio links; playlists, likes and publication choices; reports and support correspondence. Your access code is stored securely on your device and sent over HTTPS for authentication. We do not ask for contacts, precise location, microphone recordings or photo-library access in this version. Downloads and sharing happen only when you choose them.

Why we use it

We use this information to authenticate you, generate and play audio, maintain your library and credit allowance, recover pending jobs, respond to reports and support requests, and protect the service against misuse. Cloudflare and network providers may process IP addresses, request metadata and operational logs to deliver and secure the service. Prompts and API credentials are not intentionally written to application error logs.

Generation providers and hosting

Cloudflare hosts application metadata and service delivery. FAL processes the prompt, lyrics, settings and media URLs needed for the model you select, and routes them to the relevant model provider. Provider processing, permitted uses and retention vary by model. See FAL's privacy policy at https://fal.ai/privacy. We do not promise that providers never retain inputs or use them for training; do not submit confidential or personal information you cannot share with these providers. Provider and infrastructure processing can occur outside your country.

Public content and audio links

Only tracks and playlists you explicitly publish appear in public discovery. A private library entry restricts access through OpenMusic, but current beta audio is hosted on the generation provider's CDN. Anyone holding a provider audio link may be able to access it, and links may expire. Download audio you wish to keep. Sharing a downloaded file gives the recipients their own copy.

Advertising and data sales

This version has no advertising SDK, cross-app advertising tracking, in-app purchases or paid subscription flow. We do not sell your personal data. Data is disclosed to service providers to operate the features you use, when you publish or share content, or when required by law or necessary to address abuse and security incidents.

Retention and deletion

While your account is active we retain your library and job records to provide the service. In-app account deletion immediately revokes access, unpublishes content and erases your account label, prompt/lyrics content, track audio links, playlist content and your report details from our active application store. Minimal pseudonymous job and credit records are retained for settlement, fraud prevention and operational audit; pending jobs may finish, but their results are not added back to a deleted account. Deletion does not remove files held on provider systems, previously downloaded/shared copies or third-party caches. Provider-held audio follows the provider's retention and deletion procedures. Contact support for assistance with provider deletion requests.

Your controls

You can choose whether to publish, remove tracks, sign out, download your music, or delete your account from Profile. You can also request access, correction or deletion through our Account deletion page or saurav@xagi.in. Never email an access code or API key. We may ask for safe proof of account ownership.

Age and changes

OpenMusic is intended for people aged 18 or older. Do not provide personal information about children. We update this page when the service's practices change; the effective date identifies the current version.
