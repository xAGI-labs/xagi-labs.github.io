Account deletion

Control your account and content.

Updated 7 October 2026

Delete from the app

Open OpenMusic → Profile → Delete account and confirm the action. Access is revoked immediately, your tracks and playlists are unpublished, and personal account content is erased from our active application store.

Request deletion without the app

Email saurav@xagi.in with the subject “OpenMusic account deletion”. Include your account label or non-secret user ID and the contact address used to request an invitation. Do not include your access code or API key. We will confirm the request and may ask for safe proof of ownership before processing it.

What is erased

Your account label, prompts and lyrics in stored jobs/tracks, audio links in your library, playlist names/descriptions and entries, likes and report details are erased. Credentials are revoked. Pending jobs may settle, but their output is not restored to the deleted account.

What may remain

Minimal pseudonymous job/credit audit records remain to settle provider charges and prevent fraud. Provider-hosted files, copies already shared/downloaded and third-party caches are outside OpenMusic's direct deletion control. Contact support for help requesting deletion from the provider. Provider retention policies apply.

Track-only removal

You can remove individual tracks through Track options → Delete without closing your account. Track removal hides and unpublishes the entry; provider-hosted files may remain under provider retention. For a wider data deletion request, contact support.
