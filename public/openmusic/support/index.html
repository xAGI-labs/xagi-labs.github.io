Get help

For OpenMusic support, invitation requests, credit allowance questions or bug reports, email saurav@xagi.in. Include the app version, a description of the issue and a non-secret job or track ID if available. Do not send your access code, API key or confidential prompts.

Current beta

OpenMusic is currently invitation-only for creation and personal libraries. Models and generation can be temporarily unavailable. There are no subscriptions or credit purchases in this version. A download is the best way to keep audio while provider links can expire.

Report a track

Inside the app, open Track options → Report and describe your concern. You can report public tracks and your own generated output. For urgent safety concerns or copyright claims email support with the track ID, the issue and information needed to establish your rights.

Account and data

Profile includes Privacy policy, Terms of use and Delete account. If you cannot sign in, use our Account deletion page to request help. Never share an invitation code publicly.
