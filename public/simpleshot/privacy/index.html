SimpleShot privacy policy

How information is handled when you use SimpleShot on iPhone or iPad.

Effective September 25, 2026

Who provides SimpleShot

SimpleShot is provided by xAGI Labs. This policy covers the iPhone and iPad app and these SimpleShot support pages. Contact saurav@xagi.in for privacy questions or requests.

Your projects and media

The editor stores project information, imported media and editing data on your device. Editing operations such as arranging and trimming clips use that local data. Local storage does not mean every feature is offline: optional services described below can send information over the internet. Device backups, iCloud files and copies exported to other apps are also subject to the settings and policies of those services.

Optional AI and online features

If you choose an AI or generative-media feature, the selected provider can receive your prompt, relevant conversation or project context, and media needed for that request, such as audio, images or video. Configured services may include OpenAI, Anthropic, ElevenLabs, KieAI or a compatible endpoint you choose. Provider authentication information is used to authorize those requests.

The native app asks for permission before covered content-bearing AI and generation requests. AI permission can apply to a provider and purpose for the current app session. You can revoke session permissions in Settings → General. Revoking permission does not recall information already sent or stop a request already in progress. Each provider has its own processing, retention and account policies; review them before using the feature.

Templates are saved locally in this iOS release; the upstream public template gallery and template uploads are disabled. Remote assets, model downloads and provider status or model-list requests may contact their hosting services. Those services receive normal connection information, such as your IP address and request details. OpenReel-hosted services are used for some online resources.

Exports and sharing

Exported files are created locally. When you use the iOS share sheet, the destination you select determines where the file goes. If you use a cloud-upload feature, the selected media is sent to that service after the native app’s upload permission prompt. Copies held by recipients or external services are governed by their policies.

Permissions and credentials

Camera or microphone access is used when you choose to capture media. File or photo access lets you select media or save a result. Manage device permissions in iOS or iPadOS Settings. If you configure provider credentials, treat them as sensitive, remove them when no longer needed, and revoke them with the provider if they may have been exposed.

Analytics, support and website access

The native app disables the upstream PostHog analytics client. That does not prevent requests needed for optional online features. If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to include. We use them to respond, investigate issues and handle your request.

These support and privacy pages do not set analytics cookies, load advertising trackers or include a contact form. Cloudflare hosts the pages and may process connection and security information to deliver and protect them. Your email provider processes email you send to us.

Retention and deletion

Local project data remains until you delete it or remove app data. Removing the app may remove its local projects; keep copies of work you need. Separately exported files and device backups must be managed in the relevant app or backup service.

For support correspondence or information held by xAGI Labs, email us to request access, correction or deletion. We retain information as needed to address the request, maintain security and meet applicable obligations. Information sent to a third-party provider is subject to that provider’s retention rules; contact the provider for account-level deletion requests. We cannot delete copies held only on your device or by recipients you selected.

Your choices and security

You can use local editing without choosing optional AI or cloud-upload features. Share only material you are authorized to share, and avoid sending sensitive information to external providers unless you understand their handling of it. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights concerning access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information. Contact us to exercise applicable rights.

Children

SimpleShot is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. Questions or requests: saurav@xagi.in. For help using the app, visit SimpleShot support.
