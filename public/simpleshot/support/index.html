SimpleShot support

A little help, so you can get back to creating.

GET IN TOUCH

We’re here to help.

For questions, bugs, feedback or privacy requests, contact the SimpleShot team at xAGI Labs.

saurav@xagi.in

Include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and what happened. A screenshot or short description of the steps can help. Please don’t send passwords, API keys or private footage.

Importing a clip

Start a project and choose your video, photo or audio file. If a file is stored in iCloud or another cloud drive, download it to your device first. This version uses device-supported media formats and exports MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio. Unsupported formats need to be converted before importing. If an import fails, try a short H.264/AAC MP4 clip and check that your device has free storage.

Exporting and sharing

Keep the app open while exporting. Longer videos and higher resolutions need more time and free space. If an export fails, try a shorter section or a lower resolution, and tell us which settings you used. Once the system share sheet opens, choose where to save or send the video.

Looking after your projects

Keep copies of original media and export important work. Removing the app or clearing its data may remove locally stored projects. Files you saved to Photos, Files or another service are managed separately.

Optional AI features

Features that use an external AI service require that service to be available and may require your own provider account or API key. Review the destination and permission prompt before sharing content. Never include an API key in a support message.

Open-source software

SimpleShot is based on the MIT-licensed OpenReel project. It uses MediaBunny 1.25.3 under the Mozilla Public License 2.0, with a conversion callback modification. Download the modified MediaBunny source, patch and license. The download does not include your projects or media.

Privacy and data requests

Read our privacy policy for details about local storage, optional services and your choices. For a data request, email saurav@xagi.in with “SimpleShot privacy” in the subject.
