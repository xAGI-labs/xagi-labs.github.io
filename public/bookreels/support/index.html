BookReels support

Get help

Email saurav@xagi.in with the subject BookReels support. Include your app version, Android version and a description of the issue. Avoid sending passwords or sensitive personal information.

Playback

BookReels streams stories over the internet. Check your connection and try reopening the episode. You can switch English and Hindi narration and turn subtitles on or off in the player.

Remove local data

Android Settings → Apps → BookReels → Storage → Clear storage removes saved stories, progress, preferences and caches. Uninstalling also removes app-local data. This release has no account to delete. For privacy requests, contact saurav@xagi.in.

Content reports

Send the book, series and episode name when reporting an attribution, translation or content concern.
