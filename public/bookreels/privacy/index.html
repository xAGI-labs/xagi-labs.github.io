Privacy policy

About this policy

Effective October 7, 2026. BookReels is operated by Saurav Tomar under the xAGI Labs brand. This policy covers the Android guest release and its story-streaming service. Contact saurav@xagi.in for privacy questions.

Your device and accounts

This release has no sign-up or account creation. Saved stories, playback progress, narration language, subtitle preferences and speed are stored on your device. These preferences are not synced to our servers. Artwork and catalogue responses may be cached locally. Clear Android app storage or uninstall BookReels to remove this local data.

Network requests and technical information

BookReels requests story catalogues, images, subtitles and audio over HTTPS. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard connection and request metadata, which can include IP address, requested URL, timing, status and technical error information, to deliver, maintain and secure the service. We do not use this information for advertising or to build personal profiles. Operational logs may be retained under the hosting service configuration and are removed through its retention cycle; security or legally required records may be retained longer.

Other providers

Some public-domain audiobook content is served by LibriVox and Internet Archive, which receive network requests when their content is used. Opening source links or support pages also contacts the destination website. Their privacy policies govern their processing. Cloudflare acts as our hosting service provider. We do not sell personal data, use advertising SDKs, or share data for cross-app advertising.

AI illustrations and narration

Story illustrations and narration are generated during editorial production. The Android app does not send your listening activity, personal information, files or prompts to an AI generation service.

Support and retention

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and resolve the issue. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and meet legal obligations. You may request access, correction or deletion by emailing saurav@xagi.in with the subject BookReels privacy. We may need to verify the request. We cannot delete records controlled independently by third-party content providers.

Children and content

Classic stories may contain violence, coercion, historical language and adult themes. Preview stories before sharing them with children. This release does not request names, birth dates, precise location, contacts, camera or microphone access. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can address it.

Security, international processing and updates

We use HTTPS and reasonable safeguards. Hosting and content providers may process information outside your country. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Changes to this policy will appear here with an updated effective date. Your applicable privacy rights remain unaffected.
