# Dataset readiness discussion checklist

- [ ] Source identity and authorized rights holder identified
- [ ] Collection purpose and permission evidence documented
- [ ] Third-party rights, privacy and confidentiality restrictions identified
- [ ] Coverage, formats, metadata and quality limitations described
- [ ] Proposed model uses and restrictions specified
- [ ] Representative pilot and acceptance checks proposed
- [ ] Secure sample review agreed before transfer

This is a preparation tool, not legal clearance, certification or valuation. Discuss a source at https://xagi.in/data-partnerships/.
