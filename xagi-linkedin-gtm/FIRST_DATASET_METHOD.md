# First dataset sourcing method

Research checkpoint: 17 October 2026. A conversational Hindi–English speech pilot is the proposed first collection for ASR and voice-agent evaluation. This is a recommendation, not acquired inventory or confirmed buyer demand.

Primary market evidence: [Deepgram language support](https://developers.deepgram.com/docs/models-languages-overview/), [ElevenLabs India](https://elevenlabs.io/india), and [Shaip Hindi catalog](https://www.shaip.com/offerings/speech-data-catalog/hindi-dataset/). Product investment and supplier inventory do not establish an active purchase requirement.

Start with a supplier-authorized evaluation sample and documented rights before buyer distribution. A standard training-data purchase can prohibit resale: [Defined.ai published licence](https://defined.ai/data-license-agreement). Use a negotiated supplier-direct referral arrangement or an express reseller licence. An NDA does not grant distribution authority.

Record original collection method, accepted duration, transcript and speaker-turn quality, recording-linked permissions, permitted sample recipients/use and any exclusions. Keep ASR/conversational use separate from identifiable voice synthesis/cloning. Current privacy-law commencement and actual contracts need review before execution.

Private supplier drafts and sample/right templates remain local. No outreach, data transfer, payment, agreement or recording has occurred. No application source changed; application builds are not applicable.
