## About

xAGI Data Labs (xAGI Labs) helps AI teams source, acquire and license specialized training datasets by connecting model developers with data owners and collection partners.

We focus on speech and multilingual audio, video and multimodal data, and specialized datasets that are difficult to source. We also help scope custom data collection and annotation projects.

For AI teams: share the modality, use case, languages, geography, volume, quality requirements, intended usage rights and timeline. We identify potential sources and coordinate feasibility, sample evaluation, licensing discussions and delivery planning.

For data owners and suppliers: explore commercial licensing or collection partnerships for datasets you own or are authorized to license. Rights, provenance, participant consent where relevant, privacy requirements and permitted uses are discussed before any transfer.

Our work spans:
• AI training data sourcing and dataset acquisition
• Commercial dataset licensing
• Speech, voice and multilingual audio datasets
• Video and multimodal training data
• Custom data collection and annotation coordination
• Requests for Data (RFDs) and supplier discovery

Built on xAGI Labs’ background in AI engineering, voice agents and product development.
