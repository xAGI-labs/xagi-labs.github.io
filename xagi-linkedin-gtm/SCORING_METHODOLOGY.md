# Evidence-based prospect scoring
Scores are conservative earned-point totals out of 100, not probabilities of purchase or an assessment that inventory is legally cleared. Unknown information earns no points and remains explicitly unknown. A title indicates potential routing or authority, not verified procurement authority.

Buyer weights: requirements 30, model/product 25, authority 20, accessibility 15, warm/YC network 10.
- Requirements: 30 explicit relevant intake; 25 accessible older intake or historical buying with current constraints; 20 documented prior licensing partnership; 15 specific training modality/process; 10 product fit only; 5 indirect channel fit. No confirmed live budgets in this package.
- Product: 25 actual modality-relevant model development; 20 mixed platform/provider role.
- Authority: 15 founder/CEO/CTO/COO; 10 data/partnership leadership; 5 research/MTS routing. Never 20 without verified procurement authority.
- Accessibility: 10 explicit partner intake; 5 public commercial route; 0 unknown.
- Warm: 8 observed first-degree connection; 0 for second-degree/mutuals alone, unverified YC link, or unknown relationship. Relationship usefulness still must be established.
- Fit bands: High ≥65, Medium 45–64, Low <45. A routing contact may retain Medium even with higher score if authority is absent.

Supplier weights: relevance 25, licensing readiness 30, quality 20, delivery 15, price transparency 10.
- Relevance: 25 core requested modality; 20 partial/supporting; 15 peripheral service fit.
- Readiness: 25 explicit AI licensing plus rights/consent claims; 20 licensing advertised; 15 catalog/custom collection or stated permissive open license; 10 commercial services or unreviewed corpus terms. Never 30 without source-specific review.
- Quality: 15 described QA/research/governance methods; 10 basic capability claim. Claims are vendor-reported, not independent audit.
- Delivery: 15 demonstrated global/scaled services; 10 published capability; 5 catalog/community route.
- Pricing: 10 known free open-data access; 0 no public relevant quote. Published transcription/API prices are not dataset licensing prices.

Required before Qualified: verify current role, company HQ eligibility, actual buyer requirement, training/evaluation use, ownership/license chain, consent, samples and acceptance checks, delivery, and commercial terms. For stock media, ordinary stock usage rights do not establish AI training or sublicense rights. Open data is not exclusive paid inventory.

Pipeline: Identified → Qualified → Contacted → Replied → Discovery → Proposal → Negotiation → Won/Lost.
