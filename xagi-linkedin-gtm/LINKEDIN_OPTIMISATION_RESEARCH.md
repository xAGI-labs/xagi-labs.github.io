# LinkedIn optimisation for an AI data sourcing founder

Research date: 2026-10-17. Recommendations are tailored to a founder connecting AI teams with dataset owners and collection partners. Research findings are distinguished from proposed experiments; no ranking or revenue outcome is guaranteed.

## Findings from current sources

1. **Make the offer searchable and understandable.** LinkedIn recommends leading the company overview with relevant terms and completing organisation details and a goal-aligned button. xAGI's new company copy already covers training data sourcing, commercial licensing, speech, video and multimodal datasets. [LinkedIn Pages best practices](https://business.linkedin.com/advertise/linkedin-pages/best-practices).
2. **Use firsthand expertise instead of generic AI commentary.** LinkedIn's March 2026 feed update describes semantic ranking informed by profile information and interests. Its May/June update says generic, repetitive AI content and automated comments may receive less distribution. Publish useful evaluation frameworks grounded in your actual experience. [March feed update](https://news.linkedin.com/2026/ImprovingTheFeed), [June authenticity update](https://news.linkedin.com/2026/keeping-conversations-real-on-linkedin).
3. **Test document posts and practical diagrams.** Metricool's April 2026 study analysed 673,658 posts from 63,108 accounts across January-February 2025 and 2026. It reports strong performance for carousels and multi-image content, while video adoption exceeded its performance. This is observational data from connected accounts, not proof that a format will cause growth for xAGI. [Study and methodology](https://metricool.com/press-release-linkedin-study-2026/).
4. **Address the whole buying group.** The 2025 Edelman-LinkedIn research surveyed nearly 2,000 professionals and highlights hidden stakeholders. For data acquisition, useful content should serve researchers and engineers as well as procurement, legal, privacy and security reviewers. The mapping to these xAGI roles is our inference. [Research](https://www.edelman.com/expertise/Business-Marketing/2025-b2b-thought-leadership-report).
5. **Give viewers explicit paths.** LinkedIn supports three website links in profile Contact info. A homepage plus buyer and supplier links makes the two-sided offer easier to act on. [Official instructions](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a546968).
6. **Keep cover text away from edges.** LinkedIn's current official company image specification recommends 1512 x 256 pixels and a maximum 3 MB PNG/JPEG. It warns that responsive cropping can trim images. The generated master has padded artwork to support the native cropper. [Current image specifications](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a563309/image-specifications-for-your-linkedin-pages-and-career-pages?lang=en).

## Four-week measurement plan

Use the founder profile to explain decisions and practical lessons; use the company page for reusable checklists, sourcing process and partner information. This division is a proposed experiment, not a claim that personal profiles always outperform company pages.

Publish during two repeatable time windows that overlap your intended buyers' working hours, then compare results. There is no universal best posting time for this audience. Reply personally to relevant questions; do not use engagement pods, automated comments, or generic replies.

Track weekly search appearances, profile views, company visitors, relevant follower growth, post clicks, website sessions attributable to LinkedIn, qualified buyer briefs, qualified suppliers and discovery calls. Keep raw likes separate from commercially meaningful outcomes. Define a qualified buyer as an identified organisation with a concrete use case, modality, requirements and next step; define a qualified supplier as someone with identifiable source data and authority to discuss licensing. Neither definition establishes legal clearance or budget.

Do not infer uplift from the first day's observations: profile view, search appearance and impression cards may use different windows. Compare the same metric and time window over four weeks. Add UTM parameters to new campaign links only once the website analytics setup can capture them; retain clean canonical URLs in the profile.

## Draft 1 — founder post: useful sourcing method

A dataset sourcing brief needs more than a modality and a target volume.

Before evaluating speech, video or multimodal data, I would separate three questions:

1. Does the data match the task? Languages, geography, content, recording conditions and diversity matter alongside volume.
2. Can the proposed usage be licensed? Source, provenance and authority to license need to be explained, with consent and privacy requirements addressed where relevant.
3. Can a sample predict delivery quality? Define acceptance criteria before scaling collection or acquisition.

At xAGI Data Labs, we help AI teams turn these requirements into a sourcing conversation with potential dataset owners and collection partners.

If you are planning a data acquisition, the most useful starting point is a short brief with your use case, modality, languages, geography, target volume, intended rights and timeline.

https://xagi.in/ai-dataset-sourcing/

Draft only. Review against your actual process before publication. No customer, delivery or model-performance result is claimed.

## Draft 2 — supplier post

Do you own a speech, video or specialised dataset that could be useful for AI training?

A productive first conversation starts with the source and the rights, rather than sending the files.

Describe what the dataset contains, how it was collected, who controls the rights, the languages and geography, and the uses you are authorised to discuss. A metadata summary is enough to begin.

xAGI Data Labs connects AI teams with potential data owners and collection partners. We discuss feasibility, sample evaluation and commercial licensing before any data transfer.

https://xagi.in/data-partnerships/

Draft only. This is an invitation to discuss fit, not a statement that all submitted data can be licensed.

**Six-slide document outline:**
