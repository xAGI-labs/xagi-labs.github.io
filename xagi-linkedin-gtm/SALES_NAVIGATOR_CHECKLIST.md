# Sales Navigator configuration checklist
Confirm active access before creating lists; never activate trials or purchase through this workflow. If a subscription gate appears, record the blocker and continue independent evidence-based research. Inspect existing lists/searches to avoid duplicates.

Buyer account lists: B01 — Voice AI Buyers (30); B02 — Frontier AI Labs (20); B03 — Robotics AI Buyers (20); B04 — Video & Multimodal AI (20); B05 — Emerging AI Startups (30). Verify US headquarters; prioritize the Bay Area; generally headcount 11–500 with appropriate larger labs.

Supplier account lists: S01 — Speech Data Suppliers; S02 — Video Data Suppliers; S03 — Multilingual Data Partners; S04 — Data Collection Agencies; S05 — Academic & Research Data. Global. Keep commercial readiness distinct from published collection services or open research data.

Lead lists: L01 — Priority AI Data Buyers; L02 — Dataset Owners & Suppliers.

Buyer searches: voice AI ML hiring; data acquisition hiring; speech model developers; robotics foundation model startups; multimodal research companies. Supplier searches: speech licensing; audio collection; multilingual speech; video licensing; training-data collection agencies. Use only supported filters; validate actual hiring with current jobs; verify saved searches and alerts in the interface.

Stages: Identified → Qualified → Contacted → Replied → Discovery → Proposal → Negotiation → Won/Lost. Unknown budgets and inventory must remain unknown. Do not mark drafts Contacted.
