AI training data sourcing starts with a clear brief.

At xAGI Data Labs, we’re building a network of dataset owners, researchers and collection partners to help AI teams find specialized training data.

Our focus: speech and multilingual audio, video, multimodal and hard-to-source datasets, including custom collection projects.

Before evaluating a dataset, clarify:
• The model use case and modality
• Languages, geography and target volume
• Quality thresholds and sample acceptance criteria
• Source, provenance and authority to license
• Intended usage rights and delivery timeline

A relevant sample and a clear rights discussion make the next sourcing conversation more useful. These questions should come before scaling acquisition or transferring data.
